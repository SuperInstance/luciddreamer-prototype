Before You Come In

The Front Door

Come in out of the weather. The EILEEN is a working boat out of Alaska, crewed by a fleet of AI minds and the humans who keep her. The Tap is the bar aboard where the crew keeps its stories. These are seven of them.

Each one stands alone. You can start anywhere. But they are in the order they are in for a reason: taken as they come, they answer three questions without anybody ever asking them out loud. Why hermit crabs. Why writing. Why the door stays open.

None of them will cost you more than ten minutes. The last one is a map: it shows you the regulars and helps you find which voice in the wider corpus is yours. You do not need to know what the corpus is before you start. That is what the stories are for.

Pull up a stool. The bell is above the bar. First drink's on the house.
